CineRig 1.5.2 | World of Warcraft Retail | Windows and macOS

Exit WoW. Unzip the CineRig folder into World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns.
Verify AddOns/CineRig/CineRig.toc exists. Enable CineRig at character selection.
For CineRig-driven camera moves, disable DynamicCam on the character and reload.

RIG CONTROLS
Type /cine rig to open four pages. Each page also opens by name.
Motion     Set close and wide camera-distance marks and choose a pull speed.
           Hold Glance left or right and release to return. For a repeating
           move, choose a Shot, cycle count and turnaround hold, then Play.
Playback   Select a named recording, set 0.25x–2x speed or reverse it.
           Apply an In/Out range in seconds. Save excerpt creates a reusable
           named copy. Reset playback returns to its full forward performance.
Framing    Preview vertical 9:16, portrait 4:5 or square 1:1 with a matte.
           Set action and title safe margins and an eyeline height. Apply
           positions. Capture-safe and hidden UI suppress these guides.
Markers    Start a log when you start recording. Bind Drop filming marker
           for hidden-UI time stamps. Finish the log and select it to copy.
           The clock measures time since Start log and runs independently of OBS.

RECORD AND REUSE A MOVE ON A HIDDEN-UI WALK
1. Bind Toggle walking session, Start or finish movement recording, Replay
   selected movement recording and the Operator or Nudge controls you need.
2. Frame the subject, release mouse-look and type /cine record Bridge pass.
   Use an unused name. You can also enter the name in /cine recordings and
   click Record movement. Press the walking key to hide the UI.
3. Walk with the movement keys. Hold Pan right, release it, wait a beat,
   then tap a tilt or zoom Nudge. The first gesture starts the clock.
   Combined directions, ramps and pauses become part of the recording.
4. Press the recording binding to finish and save. Finish recording in the
   panel also saves a pre-named take. It saves automatically at 60 seconds.
5. Frame another subject and press Replay selected movement recording, or
   type /cine replay Bridge pass with the UI visible. Walk the route yourself.
   Pan and tilt work from your current angle. A push-in needs room to zoom.

Emergency Stop or manual camera takeover leaves a partial performance as a
draft. Save it with /cine save Gate rehearsal before another move or reload.
To name a take afterward, start with /cine record and finish with the same
command or recording key. The library holds 24 movements across characters.
Mouse-look, wheel zoom and character travel stay under your control. Start
and stop your video recording in OBS.

Open /cine presets before a hidden walk. Choose Look up and return for a
first-person tilt under an arch, or Widening arc to pan right and pull back
from a close third-person frame. Press Play while walking. The nine presets
also include Glance, Whip pan and Sideways reveal in each direction, and
Tower reveal. Append to timeline lets you edit their stages in Director.

FILM A WALK
1. In Options > Keybindings, bind CineRig's Toggle walking session and Stop.
   Bind Toggle viewfinder mouse-look and the Nudge directions you want.
   Scroll fully in for first person, or pull back to frame a third-person shot.
   Set up your cursor and subject before rolling (see below).
2. Press the walking key or type /cine walk. You can also click Walk with UI
   hidden in the Shot tab. The UI hides. Check OBS, then start recording there.
3. Walk, run, turn with the mouse and adjust the wheel normally. To hide the
   on-screen pointer, hold your normal mouse-look button (right mouse by
   default) or tap Toggle viewfinder mouse-look. Physically steer with the
   mouse. Press the viewfinder key again to return the pointer. Stop and
   ending the walk also release mouse-look that CineRig still owns.
4. For a small pan, release mouse-look and tap a Nudge key while walking with
   the movement keys. Hold an Operator key for a longer move. Native mouse
   steering can take over from the rig while the walking UI stays hidden.
5. Stop OBS, then press the walking key again. Stop, Restore or /cine reset
   also ends the session. If CineRig hid the UI, it brings it back. If you hid
   the UI yourself before walking, show it with WoW's Toggle User Interface.

For a timed walk, use First-person walkthrough or Third-person walkthrough
in Shot. Each gives you 15 seconds by default to steer and zoom freely. For a
slow third-person pan as you walk with the movement keys, select Walking arc.
OBS records your route. Named CineRig recordings capture Operator and Nudge
camera commands.

CURSOR AND CHARACTER
For a free mouse during a pan, tilt or zoom, open /cine cursor and switch
Hide free cursor (experimental) on for this login. Start /cine walk or play
a capture-safe shot. Keep mouse-look off and use your camera keybindings.
Move the mouse across bright scenery while you record all three directions.
Check the clip with your usual OBS settings. Keep the game in the foreground.
Stop or show the interface to recover your pointer.

If WoW refuses a camera Stop or a cursor cleanup, press Restore. CineRig also
retries outstanding cleanup when combat ends or you enter the world, while
respecting any intervening camera or cursor handoff.

This mode temporarily uses WoW's software pointer and a transparent image.
The separate Hardware pointer on walks option is for capture sources that
exclude hardware cursors. The free-cursor effect takes priority while active.

For a third-person landscape without your character, frame the shot first.
Open Monitor > Capture or /cine subject, click Hide character, then press
Enter in chat to submit /console showPlayer 0. Escape cancels the draft.
Check your character is out of frame. After the take, show the UI, click
Show character and press Enter, or type /console showPlayer 1 and press
Enter. This explicit Show step returns your character. Capture also reports
your current perspective and distance.

For the Stormwind reveal, bind Play and Restore. Type /cine. Stand still,
zoom out several steps, frame the gate and choose Play shot. The reveal runs
eight seconds after a three-second count. Reframe manually before replaying.
Try Stop midway through a second take. After /reload the rig starts idle.

SHOT       Choose a preset with < and >. Change duration, peak rate, direction,
           ease or countdown. Push/pull changes camera distance.
MOVES      Compose offers nine presets including Orbit and push, Rising
           reveal and Descending push. Adjust Pan, Tilt and Distance
           separately. Click a center value to zero it. Set duration and
           easing, then Play shot. Use Director > Library > Save shot to keep
           your version. In Nudges, set Travel to Micro, Small or Medium
           and duration to 0.6, 1 or 1.6 seconds. The six one-tap bindings
           pan left/right, tilt up/down, and push in/pull back.
OPERATOR   Bind hold directions, precision and live speed. Use Arm, hold keys
           to perform a move, release and let the ramp finish. Save take
           replaces this character's one live slot. Replay after reframing.
DIRECTOR   Add the selected shot to the timeline. Trim seconds from either end,
           add a hold, select cut/ease, reorder, preview a segment or loop.
           Library saves named shots/takes. Transfer copies authored takes.
MONITOR    Rehearsal shows optional guides and take labels. Capture-safe hides
           the game interface when you roll. A finished authored take or live
           replay keeps the UI hidden for post-roll. Stop or Restore brings
           back an interface CineRig hid. If you hid the UI with your native
           Toggle User Interface binding, use that binding to show it again.

Check the actual recording. Guides visible during rehearsal can appear in OBS.
For a physical dolly, walk forward or backward. For a sideways tracking shot,
use your strafe keys. Orbit your character in third person by holding an
Operator pan key. For a crane rise, descent or flyover, mount before rolling,
then pilot the path and altitude yourself where flight is available.

This release targets Retail 12.1.5.70961, interface 120105. Check camera
feel, collision, physical controls and cursor visibility in a short clip.
Use /cine diag for the current build, supported axes, owner and recent errors.
The companion source distribution includes README.md, the Retail smoke test,
the API capability matrix, changelog, Lua tests and the packaging tool.

To remove CineRig, Stop, Restore and use Show character + Enter if you hid
your character. Exit WoW and move AddOns/CineRig out of the game.
To roll back, restore your previous addon folder and its matching SavedVariables
backup. WoW saves CineRig.lua in the account and character SavedVariables folders.
Preserve both files if you want to keep your shots and live take.
